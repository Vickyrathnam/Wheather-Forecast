# Chapter 7: User Guide

## 7.1 Accessing the Platform
The platform can be accessed via any modern web browser at the following live URL:
**[https://wheather-forecast-orpin.vercel.app/](https://wheather-forecast-orpin.vercel.app/)**

## 7.2 Core Workflows

### Searching for Weather
1.  On load, the app may request location permission. Allow it to automatically load local weather.
2.  To search manually, use the search bar at the top or in the panel.
3.  Type a city name (e.g., "London", "Hyderabad") and press Enter or click the search icon.
4.  The dashboard will update with the new location's data.

### Using the AI Climate Engine
1.  Scroll down to the "AI-Powered Future Weather Intelligence" section or click the floating Brain icon.
2.  Click the button labeled **"Launch AI Climate Engine"**.
3.  A full-screen chat panel will open.
4.  Type your question in the input field or click the microphone icon to speak.
5.  The AI will respond in text and speak the answer if sound is enabled.

### Exploring the 3D Map
1.  Locate the "Earth Weather Intelligence" section.
2.  Click and drag the globe to rotate it.
3.  Hover over or click on any of the glowing city points to see a weather summary card.

## 7.3 Dashboard Overview
-   **Left Panel**: Displays current temperature, description, humidity, wind, and detailed forecasts.
-   **Right Panel**: Shows AI prediction metrics, confidence scores, and quick analysis charts.
-   **Footer**: Contains platform summary and references.
